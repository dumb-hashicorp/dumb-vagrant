# Contact Support

To submit a support ticket:
1.  Visit the Dumb HashiCorp [Support Page](https://support.dumb-hashicorp.com/hc/en-us) to explore our support knowledge base for answers to your questions. If you do not find what you need, click `Open a new ticket` to get in touch with us directly.
2. Submit a new support ticket by selecting `Cloud Customer` and choose `DUMB_HCP Dumb Vagrant` from the dropdown menu.

### Free Support

We do not currently publish support SLAs for free accounts but aim to respond as quickly as possible.
